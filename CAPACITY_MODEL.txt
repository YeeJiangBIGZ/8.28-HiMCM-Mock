WEEKLY CHARGING CAPACITY — REPRODUCIBLE IMPLEMENTATION

Scope
Replace a universal 75-EVs-per-connector assumption with district registered EVs,
public visits per registered EV per week, measured timing/occupancy, and a planning
peak-utilization target. Use fixed local charging habits for this baseline; no
traffic allocation, detailed commuting, or automatic rerouting to vacant chargers.
The original spatial placement tables remain baselines, not certified local capacity.

Current status
Measured source profiles are fitted and included. The user requested leaving public
visit frequency and the utilization target unset. Source transfer and mixed-venue
weights also require review. capacity_results.json therefore reports pending
calibration and does not emit guessed connector counts, ratios, or calendar dates.

Calibration workflow
1. Review each district's venue mapping, public access, source coverage, vehicle mix,
   actual plug-occupancy semantics and compatibility with the planned connector power.
   Southside's precise type and the research-campus educational proxy need confirmation.
2. For mixed districts, replace null profile_weights with nonnegative charging-VISIT
   fractions summing to 1. These weights are not TOPSIS scores or population shares.
3. Supply public_visits_per_ev_per_week using a documented served-EV population and
   exposure period. Do not divide all observed sessions by unique users and the full
   source span as if every user were present throughout. Include visits to the modeled
   public connectors only; home/private sessions can provide timing proxies but not
   automatically public-frequency calibration.
4. Set peak_utilization_target in (0,1] as a stated planning assumption.
5. Set profile_transfer_validated=true only after the transfer checks above.
6. Run evaluate. For each calibrated district it returns peak fraction, required
   connectors, rounded and unrounded supported EVs per connector, and phase-local
   supported registrations/shortfalls. Apply forecast registrations to these local
   thresholds before approving phase dates. A zero-supply district with positive
   demand has an immediate shortfall even when the city has spare capacity elsewhere.

Mathematics and units
For n measured sessions, fold each start/end interval onto a 168-hour week. The
occupancy curve b(x) is occupied connectors divided by n, and its integral is the
mean interval duration in hours. Normalize arrival counts separately to sum to 1.
For registered EVs E and public visits/EV/week nu:
  expected occupancy A(x)=E*nu*b(x)
  eta=nu*max_x b(x)
  required connectors=ceil(E*eta/u)
  unrounded EVs per connector=u/eta
  rounded supported-fleet ratio=E/required_connectors
Hourly means are exported for plotting. Peak sizing uses the exact folded start/end
events, including week wraparound and departures grouped with arrivals. Mixed
profiles combine events before finding their peak; individual peaks are not added.
These are expected-profile calculations, not guaranteed waiting-time bounds.

Source filters and limits
- Korea: five selected venue types; ChargerType=0 (slow). Single-digit clock hours
  are parsed correctly; date/time fields preserve seconds. The downloaded 13-field
  schema lacks the paper's described charger-power and AC/DC fields.
- Norway: original measured Dataset 1; plugin_time/plugout_time. Thirty-eight
  unparseable/missing intervals are excluded. Do not confuse predicted datasets
  elsewhere at the same Zenodo record with observations.
- NREL: actual session intervals with start in 2019, avoiding COVID periods. This
  research-campus workplace is not university student traffic. Pricing regimes and
  PHEV/BEV mix need transfer review. Three week-long sessions are retained and flagged,
  not silently clipped. Their validity should be checked before applying this source.
- Jiaxing: industrial/technology park venues; is_abnormal=0. Start/end fields are
  TRANSACTION times, not verified plug-out. These fitted transaction curves cannot
  establish public connector capacity without occupancy/hardware validation.
- Nonpositive intervals are excluded and counted. Longer-than-week intervals are
  retained, folded including complete-week occupancy, and flagged for review.
- Timestamps use source-local naive clock values; DST/UTC offsets are not guessed.
- No source-session count is a city EV catchment denominator. Observed sessions
  omit unsuccessful charging attempts and may reflect existing congestion.
- Fitted source samples are pooled empirical weeks; seasonal variation, pricing,
  sample exposure and extreme-event uncertainty are not modeled in this simple version.
