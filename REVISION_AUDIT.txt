Numerical verification
Run: /opt/anaconda3/bin/python -m unittest -v test_queue_model.py
Eight checks pass: zero-port Poisson accumulation, exponential completion, stationary
Erlang C agreement, block-splitting invariance, overload then recovery, additional ports,
capacity expansion, and invalid initial-state rejection. Overflow is an error monitor,
not an implemented queue limit; state range is increased until outputs converge.
Illustrative two-port case: mu=1/hour, initially empty, arrivals3/hour for4hours then
0.25/hour for12hours. Peak backlog3.963554, final backlog0.010322, total waiting16.350528
vehicle-hours, completed14.729552, in service0.260126. Conservation residual<1e-12.
These parameters/results illustrate the mathematics and are not Springfield estimates.
